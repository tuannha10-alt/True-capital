Why we exist

We built True Capital for founders
who deserve better

Too many Vietnamese business owners face their most important financial decisions without the right support. We've seen it firsthand — and it's why we do what we do.

The struggle
"I want to raise capital, but I don't even know where to start — who to talk to, what to prepare, or what my company is worth."
Our approach
We start from the very beginning — helping you understand valuation, build the right materials, and prepare your story before a single investor meeting. No jargon, no assumptions.
The struggle
"I've spent months meeting investors who weren't serious. I'm exhausted and my business is suffering from the distraction."
Our approach
We screen investors before you meet them — filtering for genuine interest, sector fit, and ticket size. Your time is spent only with people who are ready to move.
The struggle
"I feel alone in this. My accountant doesn't understand deals, my lawyer just reviews papers, and I have no one who sees the full picture."
Our approach
We become your trusted partner through the entire journey — from strategy to closing. One person who understands your business, your goals, and the deal mechanics. You're never alone in the room.
